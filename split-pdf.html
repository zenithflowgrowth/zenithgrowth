Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about security, formatting standards, and zero server upload privacy.

01. How does in-browser PDF splitting protect my privacy? ▼

Zenith executes PDF page separation locally using client-side JavaScript. Confidential financial statements, legal briefs, and contracts never touch an external server.

02. Can I extract non-consecutive pages like pages 1, 3, and 7? ▼

Yes. You can specify exact comma-separated page ranges or single pages to assemble a customized extract of your document.

03. Will splitting alter the formatting or hyperlinks in my PDF? ▼

No. Zenith preserves all native PDF objects, embedded vector fonts, high-resolution imagery, and document hyperlinks without alteration.

04. What should I do if my PDF file is password protected? ▼

If your file requires a password to open, first use our Unlock PDF tool to decrypt it, then proceed with splitting.

05. Are there any subscriptions, watermarks, or hidden fees? ▼

None. All Zenith PDF utilities are 100% free forever with no watermarks and no registration required.
