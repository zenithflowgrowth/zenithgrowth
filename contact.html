Frequently Asked Questions

Are all 16 tools really 100% free forever?

Yes. Zenith Growth is built using client-side WebAssembly, meaning it costs almost zero server compute to run. We do not charge subscriptions or lock features behind paywalls.

Can I request a new tool to be built?

Absolutely! If you have a document or media workflow you do frequently, email us at zenithflow.growth@gmail.com with your suggestion and we'll evaluate adding it.

Do you store any of my processed files?

Never. All file transformations execute in your browser's RAM and never leave your machine.
