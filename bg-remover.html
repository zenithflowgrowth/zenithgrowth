Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to external servers? ▼

No. Unlike Canva or Remove.bg which transmit your files to cloud servers, Zenith Growth executes background removal 100% locally inside your web browser. Confidential product photos, signatures, and IDs never leave your computer.

Is there a daily file limit or hidden watermark? ▼

No. Zenith Growth is 100% free with unlimited daily usage and zero watermarks. You receive high-resolution transparent PNG files directly to your device.

What image formats are supported? ▼

You can upload PNG, JPG/JPEG, and modern WebP images. Download cutouts as transparent PNGs or clean white-background JPGs.
