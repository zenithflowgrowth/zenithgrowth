Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding supported video formats, privacy, and social media exports.

01. Does Zenith Video Editor upload my confidential video clips to a remote server? ▼

No. All video rendering, color filtering, trimming, and exports take place entirely inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio, and WebAssembly. Your videos never leave your computer or phone.

02. Can I reframe horizontal videos for TikTok, Reels, and vertical feeds? ▼

Yes! Zenith features 1-click social presets including 9:16 Vertical (TikTok, Reels, Stories), 1:1 Square (Instagram Posts), 16:9 Landscape (Widescreen Displays), and 4:5 Portrait with smart centering.

03. Can I export video clips with sound or mute them? ▼

Yes. Zenith supports both 'With Sound' (original synchronized audio) and 'Mute Audio' export modes, allowing you to create silent clips or preserve high-fidelity audio.

04. Does Zenith add any watermark to exported videos? ▼

Never. All exported clips are 100% clean with zero added watermarks, branding, or logos.

05. How does the animated sprinkles and sparkles effect work? ▼

The particle engine renders real-time physics-based sparkles, confetti, and sparkles on top of your video layer at 60 FPS directly on your GPU.
