Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about security, formatting standards, and zero server upload privacy.

01. Are e-signatures created on Zenith legally valid? ▼

Yes, electronic signatures created with Zenith meet standard requirements for agreements, non-disclosure agreements, and freelance contracts under the ESIGN Act and UETA guidelines.

02. Can someone see or intercept my signature or contract? ▼

No. Unlike traditional cloud signing platforms, Zenith executes 100% in your browser. Your signature and document are never uploaded to any server.

03. Can I add typed names, dates, and initials alongside my signature? ▼

Yes, you can easily position text annotations, dates, and initials anywhere on the document before saving.

04. Can I upload a transparent PNG image of my real signature? ▼

Yes, you can upload any PNG with transparency or use the built-in signature canvas to draw directly with your mouse or stylus.

05. Is there a limit on how many contracts I can sign for free? ▼

Zero limits. Sign as many documents as you need without subscriptions or hidden paywalls.
