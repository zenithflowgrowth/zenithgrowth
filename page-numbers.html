Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about security, formatting standards, and zero server upload privacy.

01. Can I format page numbers as "Page X of Y"? ▼

Yes, choose from standard sequential numbers (1, 2, 3), "Page X", or full dynamic pagination "Page X of Y".

02. Can I exclude page numbers from the cover or title page? ▼

Yes, you can configure the start offset so numbering begins neatly on page 2 or any subsequent chapter.

03. Where on the page can I place the numbers? ▼

You can align numbers at the bottom right, bottom center, bottom left, top right, or top center with custom margins.

04. Are my uploaded documents sent to an external server? ▼

No. Pagination is calculated and stamped directly in your browser memory with zero cloud transmission.

05. Does it handle documents with hundreds of pages? ▼

Yes, the client-side engine efficiently paginates large PDF books and contracts in seconds.
