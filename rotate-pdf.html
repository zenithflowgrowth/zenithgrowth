Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about security, formatting standards, and zero server upload privacy.

01. Does rotating PDF pages alter the document text or quality? ▼

No. Rotating only updates the page viewport matrix metadata. The underlying text layers, vector curves, and image pixels remain untouched and sharp.

02. Can I rotate just one upside-down page without changing the rest? ▼

Yes! You can rotate individual pages independently or rotate all pages at once to match your desired reading layout.

03. Is the page rotation permanent when opened in Adobe Acrobat or mobile viewers? ▼

Yes. The saved PDF contains the updated standard rotation angle flags, so it displays correctly in every PDF viewer and printer.

04. Are my private files kept confidential? ▼

Absolutely. All processing occurs entirely inside your web browser. Your files never touch our servers.

05. Does Zenith Rotate PDF work on mobile devices? ▼

Yes, it works smoothly across all modern mobile and desktop browsers including Safari, Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.
