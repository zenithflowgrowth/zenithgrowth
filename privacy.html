Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Official Domain: zenithgrowth.tech

1. Our Core Principle: Zero-Data Upload

Zenith Growth (zenithgrowth.tech) was built with privacy as the core foundation. All utilities (including Merge PDF, Image to PDF, Invoice Maker, AI Cost Estimator, and JSON Formatter) execute entirely inside your web browser.

We do not transmit, log, store, or sell any PDF files, images, code snippets, or client invoices you process through our tools. Everything stays local.

2. Local Storage Usage

Certain tools (such as the Invoice Maker) utilize your browser's local storage solely so you do not lose draft work upon refreshing. This data never touches our server and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache.

3. Advertising & Cookies

To keep all tools 100% free of charge, we may display contextual third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense). These third-party providers may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For privacy inquiries or technical questions regarding zenithgrowth.tech, contact our support team via our official domain channels.
