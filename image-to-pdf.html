Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about security, formatting standards, and zero server upload privacy.

01. Which image file formats are supported? ▼

Zenith supports all major graphic formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF.

02. Can I reorder images before generating the PDF? ▼

Yes. You can drag and rearrange image cards to organize the exact sequence before compilation.

03. Does converting photos reduce image resolution or clarity? ▼

No. We preserve native image dimensions and color profiles without heavy lossy recompression.

04. Are my personal photos uploaded to remote servers? ▼

Never. Image-to-PDF compilation executes entirely inside your browser memory using client-side JavaScript.

05. Can I create standard A4 or US Letter sized PDFs? ▼

Yes, the compiler automatically fits your images into standard document dimensions with clean margins.
